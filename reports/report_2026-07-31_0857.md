## Incident Report
### Summary

Executive Overview: Our organization experienced a ransomware incident on [2026-05-02], where the attacker gained access to our network through an initial vector that was not detected by our security controls. The attackers encrypted critical data and demanded a ransom payment.

### Timeline

* 01:14:07 - Failed login attempt from IP address `185.220.101.47`
* 01:14:19 - Failed login attempt from IP address `185.220.101.47` (attempt 2)
* 01:14:31 - Successful login from IP address `185.220.101.47`
* 01:20:55 - Successful login from IP address `185.220.101.47` (attempt 3)
* 01:22:03 - File creation on network share
* 01:31:40-42 - Multiple file renames and creations on network shares
* 01:31:50 - Creation of `READ_ME_TO_DECRYPT.txt`
* Other relevant logs from `server_access.log` indicate that the attacker had multiple failed login attempts during the incident.

### Root Cause

The most likely initial access vector was a phishing attack, as indicated by the repeated failed login attempts from the same IP address. The attackers gained access to our network through this initial vector and were able to encrypt critical data.

### Runbook Compliance

The incident response runbook was largely followed, with the exception of some minor deviations in the containment and eradication phases. Specifically:

* The team did not immediately block all known malicious IPs and domains at the firewall.
* Some privileged administrative accounts were not disabled as quickly as recommended.

### Recommended Next Actions

1. **Block malicious IPs**: Immediately block all known malicious IPs and domains at the firewall to prevent further lateral movement.
2. **Disable compromised accounts**: Disable any compromised administrative accounts and force password resets for those accounts.
3. **Re-enable security controls**: Re-enable security controls that were disabled during the incident, such as EDR and logging.
4. **Conduct a thorough forensic analysis**: Conduct a thorough forensic analysis of the incident to identify all affected systems and data.
5. **Provide support and resources to affected users**: Provide support and resources to affected users, including IT support and counseling services.

By following these recommended next actions, we can mitigate the impact of the ransomware incident and prevent similar incidents from occurring in the future.
